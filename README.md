AutoCPlay 让你在车机上使用熟悉的 CarPlay 界面与操作方式。连接后，就像平时使用 CarPlay 一样：打开你习惯的应用，在车机上轻松操作。

### 为什么选择 AutoCPlay

- **熟悉好用**：熟悉的 CarPlay 使用方式，轻松上手。
- **无线便捷**：支持 Wi-Fi 连接，车机浏览器即可使用。
- **音画出色**：无损音频、流畅画面、灵敏触控，享受稳定顺畅的使用体验。
- **更多连接选择**：Wi-Fi + 浏览器、CarLink 现已支持；CarLife 即将推出。

### 下载：先装 TestFlight，再装 AutoCPlay

1. 在 iPhone 上[安装 Apple TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664)。
2. 打开 [AutoCPlay 测试邀请链接](https://testflight.apple.com/join/BuX7Uh9v)，按提示安装。也可以到[官网下载区](https://nexiumaionline.com/#download)，用 iPhone 相机扫描二维码。
3. 安装完成后，打开 AutoCPlay。后续测试版更新也可在 TestFlight 中获取。

测试名额与系统要求以 TestFlight 邀请页为准。

### 连接：三步开始

**Wi-Fi + 车机浏览器**

1. **打开 App**：在 iPhone 上打开 AutoCPlay。
2. **连接 Wi-Fi**：按 App 提示完成手机与车机的 Wi-Fi 连接。
3. **打开浏览器**：在车机浏览器中输入 App 显示的地址，连接后即可使用。车机无需安装浏览器方式的专用 App。

**CarLink（现已支持）**

在 AutoCPlay 中按 CarLink 连接提示操作，并在车机端打开 CarLink，按提示完成连接即可。不同车机的入口可能不同，具体以 App 和车机显示为准。

### 使用：和熟悉的 CarPlay 一样

连接成功后，在车机屏幕上打开应用、切换界面、使用导航和音乐，沿用你熟悉的 CarPlay 操作习惯。可用应用和功能以当前版本与设备为准。

AutoCPlay brings the familiar CarPlay interface and controls to your car display. Once connected, use it just as you normally use CarPlay: open your favorite apps and enjoy familiar controls on your car screen.

### Why AutoCPlay

- **Familiar and easy**: the CarPlay experience you already know.
- **Wireless convenience**: connect over Wi-Fi and use your car display’s browser.
- **A smooth experience**: lossless audio, fluid visuals, responsive touch, and a stable connection.
- **More ways to connect**: Wi-Fi + browser and CarLink are available now. CarLife is coming soon.

### Download: TestFlight first, then AutoCPlay

1. [Install Apple TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) on your iPhone.
2. Open the [AutoCPlay beta invitation](https://testflight.apple.com/join/BuX7Uh9v) and follow the prompts to install. You can also scan the QR code in our [website’s download section](https://nexiumaionline.com/#download) with your iPhone camera.
3. Open AutoCPlay. Future beta updates are available through TestFlight.

Beta availability and system requirements are listed on the invitation page.

### Connect in three steps

**Wi-Fi + car display browser**

1. **Open the app**: launch AutoCPlay on your iPhone.
2. **Connect Wi-Fi**: follow the app’s instructions to connect your iPhone and car display.
3. **Open the browser**: enter the address shown in AutoCPlay into your car display’s browser, then start using it. No dedicated head-unit app is needed for browser mode.

**CarLink — available now**

Follow the CarLink connection prompts in AutoCPlay, open CarLink on your car display, and complete the on-screen steps. Menu locations may vary by car display; follow the instructions shown on your devices.

### Use it like CarPlay

Once connected, open apps, switch screens, and enjoy navigation and music using the CarPlay controls you already know. Available apps and features depend on your device and the current version.

### Help and community

- **Trouble connecting?** Check Wi-Fi, reopen AutoCPlay, and make sure the address in your car browser matches the one currently shown in the app.
- **Questions about your car display?** Join [Discord](https://discord.gg/s5JQjwwDZ) and share your car display model, iPhone model, app version, and the issue.
- **Learn more and download**: [nexiumaionline.com](https://nexiumaionline.com).
